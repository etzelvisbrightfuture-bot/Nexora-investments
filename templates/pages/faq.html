Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about investing with Nexora.

How do I start investing with Nexora?

Getting started is easy! Simply click the "Sign Up" button, create your free account, and verify your email. Once logged in, navigate to the "Investments" page, choose a plan that fits your goals, and follow the payment instructions.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept a variety of secure payment methods including Cryptocurrencies (Solana, Bitcoin, Ethereum) and Gift Cards (Amazon, Apple, Steam, etc.). All payments require a screenshot proof for verification.

How long does it take for my investment to be approved?

Our finance team manually verifies all transactions to ensure maximum security. Typically, investments are approved and activated within 1 to 24 hours after submission.

When can I withdraw my profits?

You can request a withdrawal at any time once your investment is marked as "Active" or "Completed" in your dashboard. Withdrawals are processed by our admin team within 24-48 hours.

Is my money safe with Nexora?

Absolutely. We utilize bank-level encryption, cold storage for digital assets, and rigorous risk management protocols to ensure your funds are protected at all times.
